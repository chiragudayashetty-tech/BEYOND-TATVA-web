Is using AI for studies cheating? A guide for parents

By Chirag Shetty, Beyond Tatva, Udupi · 31 October 2026

Short answer: copying is cheating; using AI to understand and practise is not. The tool is the same, so what matters is how your child uses it.

Honest use

  • Asking AI to explain a confusing paragraph in simpler words
  • Making flashcards or practice questions from their own chapter
  • Getting feedback on an answer they wrote themselves
  • Brainstorming ideas for a project, then doing the work themselves

Dishonest use

  • Submitting AI-written homework, essays or project reports as their own
  • Using AI during tests or exams
  • Copying answers without understanding them

A simple test for your child

Ask: "Could you explain this answer to me without your phone?" If yes, AI helped them learn. If no, AI did the work for them.

Why banning AI doesn't work

Students will meet AI in college and at work. Teaching them to use it honestly now, while they are in Class 8–10, is safer than leaving them to figure it out alone.
