How Class 10 students can use AI to prepare for board exams (the honest way)

By Chirag Shetty, Beyond Tatva, Udupi · 31 October 2026

AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini are already on most students' phones. Used well, they act like a patient tutor available at 11pm. Used badly, they write answers your child never understands. Here is how to get the first without the second.

1. Start from the textbook, not from AI

The board exam is set from the textbook. Ask your child to paste or type a paragraph from their own chapter and ask AI to explain it more simply, rather than asking AI an open question. This keeps answers tied to the syllabus and cuts down on mistakes.

2. Turn chapters into flashcards and short notes

A good prompt: "Here is my Class 10 Science chapter section. Make 10 question-and-answer flashcards from it, using only this text." Your child should then test themselves without looking.

3. Practise with exam-style questions

Ask AI to write 1-mark, 2-mark and 5-mark questions from a chapter. Your child writes the answers by hand first, then asks AI to point out what is missing. The writing is what builds exam memory.

4. Check everything

AI can be confidently wrong, especially with numbers, dates and diagrams. Always cross-check with the textbook or a teacher.

5. Know when not to use AI

Not in exams, not for graded homework, and not for work that is supposed to be your child's own. Using AI to understand and practise is fine; copying its output is not.
