Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

We collect only what we need to run the course: the parent's or student's name, phone number, school, town and the messages you send us. For enrolled students we also store course progress.

Children

Our students are in Classes 8–10 and are under 18. We ask a parent or guardian to give consent before a student enrols, and we contact parents, not children, for anything about payments.

Before checkout, a parent or guardian must tick a consent box. We record that consent with the payment, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not track children or show them targeted ads. We message families about future courses only if the parent ticks the separate optional box, and anyone can opt out at any time.

How we use it

  • To send Module 0, course access and updates on WhatsApp or email.
  • To give parents and, where a school partners with us, the school progress updates.
  • To improve the course using anonymised usage data.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the services that run the course: Razorpay (payments), Google (forms and email), Supabase and Vercel (hosting). Payments are handled by Razorpay; we never see your card or UPI details.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us. Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message to stop updates. We delete lead data we no longer need within 12 months.
