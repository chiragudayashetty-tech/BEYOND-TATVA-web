FAQ

Questions parents ask

Will this actually help with board exams?

Yes, that's the main focus. Module 1 teaches your child to turn their own textbook chapters into revision notes, flashcards and exam-style practice questions, aligned to the NCERT and Karnataka board syllabus. They use it on what they're studying right now.

Isn't using AI for studies just cheating?

Copying answers is cheating. Using AI to understand a chapter faster or to quiz yourself is like having a patient tutor. We spend real time on when not to use AI: in exams, graded homework and independent work. Honest use is built into every module.

My child isn't good with technology. Will they manage?

Yes. No coding or prior knowledge is needed, just a phone or laptop. If they can use WhatsApp and YouTube, they can follow the course. We start from absolute basics.

How much time does it take?

It's self-paced with lifetime access, so there's no deadline. Most students finish in 1–2 weeks alongside school. The certificate is earned by completing all 5 modules.

Which classes is it for?

It's designed for Class 8–10 students, especially those preparing for SSLC and other board exams.
