FAQ

Questions parents ask

Will this actually help with board exams?

Yes, that's the main focus. Module 1 teaches your child to turn their own textbook chapters into revision notes, flashcards and exam-style practice questions, aligned to NCERT and Karnataka board syllabus.

Isn't using AI for studies just cheating?

Copying answers is cheating. Using AI to understand a chapter or quiz yourself is like having a patient tutor. We spend real time on when not to use AI: exams, graded homework and independent work.

My child isn't good with technology. Will they manage?

Yes. No coding or prior knowledge needed, just a phone or laptop. If they can use WhatsApp and YouTube, they can follow the course. We start from absolute basics.

How much time does it take?

It's self-paced with lifetime access, so there's no deadline. Most students finish in 1–2 weeks alongside school.
