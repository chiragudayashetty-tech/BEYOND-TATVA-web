# Photos for the Beyond Tatva site

Drop files into this folder with **exactly these names**. The site picks each one up
automatically. Until a file exists, a drawn artwork is shown in its place, so nothing breaks.

Use WebP (or rename a JPG to `.webp` only after converting it, e.g. at squoosh.app), under ~250 KB each.

| File name | Where it shows | Shape | What to shoot |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero.webp` | Full-width behind the hero (darkened on the left) | 16:9, 2000×1125 | Cinematic, night-time: a Class 9–10 student at a desk, lit warmly by a phone/laptop glow, textbooks open. Face on the **right half**. Real Udupi home or school feel, not stock. |
| `mentor.webp` | Mentor section (arched frame) | 4:5, 1000×1250 | Chirag, chest-up, natural light, looking at camera, warm background. |
| `workshop.webp` | Schools section | 16:10, 1600×1000 | A real workshop moment: students in uniform, hands up or looking at a screen. Get the school's permission. |
| `make-poster.webp` | "What they'll make" moodboard | 4:5 | A real student poster made with AI tools (e.g. "Save Water"). |
| `make-flashcards.webp` | Moodboard | 1:1 | Flashcards made from a textbook chapter, on a desk. |
| `make-video.webp` | Moodboard | 3:4 | A still from a student's 60-second explainer video. |
| `make-website.webp` | Moodboard | 4:3 | Screenshot of a student's personal web page. |
| `make-timetable.webp` | Moodboard | 5:4 | A colourful weekly timetable a student made. |
| `make-certificate.webp` | Moodboard | 4:5 | Your real QR certificate design, printed or on screen. |

Only use photos of children with written permission from their parents.
