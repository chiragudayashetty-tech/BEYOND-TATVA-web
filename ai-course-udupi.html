AI course for Class 8–10 students in Udupi

Beyond Tatva is an AI skills course made in Udupi for students in Class 8, 9 and 10. It teaches children to use AI the right way: to understand their own textbook chapters, practise for board exams, and build small creative projects, without copying.

Who it is for

Students in Class 8–10 (CBSE and Karnataka State Board) from Udupi, Manipal, Kundapur, Karkala, Brahmavar and nearby towns. No coding background is needed. Because the course is online and self-paced, students outside Udupi district can join too.

What your child learns

  • Module 0 (free): what AI really is, three golden rules for honest use, and a first study prompt. About 15 minutes.
  • Board-exam study skills: turning their own chapters into notes, flashcards and exam-style questions.
  • Creative projects that use AI as a helper, not a replacement.
  • Life skills and responsible use: checking facts, staying safe, and knowing when not to use AI.
